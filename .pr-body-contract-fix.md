## Scope

Portaliq #746's contract said shillinq writes `activitySignup.paymentRequestRef`; shillinq does not write into another app's register, and its contributions raise (shillinq #1704) answers with the ids instead. Portaliq now raises the contribution for an activity's confirmed places itself, through shillinq's in-process `ContributionRaiseService::raise()`, and writes each returned `paymentRequestId` into the sign-up: the `activityOffer` is the chargeable (the request's subject), the child the beneficiary, the guardian who signed up the debtor.

Decisions D19 and D30. Shillinq's contract (`openspec/changes/extracurricular-fee-to-shillinq/contract.md`, version 1, Consumers): "portaliq raises the contribution of an activityOffer for the guardians of its confirmed places, and writes its own activitySignup.paymentRequestRef from the raise response."

## What changes

- `POST /apps/portaliq/api/activities/{id}/contributions` (staff, Nextcloud session): body `amount`, `voluntary`, `administrationId`, optional `description` (default the activity title), `invoiceDate`, `dueDate`, `revenueAccount`, `language`, `currency`. Bills every `confirmed` sign-up without a reference, in chunks of 200; answers `{raised, skipped, failed, results[{signupId, childRef, status, paymentRequestRef?, reason?}]}`.
- A `skipped` answer (shillinq already billed that child for this activity) writes the standing request id too, so a retry heals a failed write and nobody is billed twice. Waitlisted and withdrawn places are never billed; a later promotion is billed on the next run.
- Debtor `{portalSubjectRef, name, email}` from the guardian's `portalAccount` (`email`, else `verifiedEmail`); without a name or email the place is reported `failed` / `no_contact_details` and not sent.
- `ShillinqContributionRaiser`: duck-typed call (no shillinq `use`, no info.xml dependency); 503 `shillinq_unavailable`, 400 `invalid_charge`, 403 `forbidden` (shillinq's `payment.request` check on the staff member), 502 `raise_failed`.
- No amount is stored in portaliq.
- #746's `contract.md`, `design.md` D4 and `proposal.md` amended in place; `docs/operations/term-long-activities.md` gains the raise.
- Register 0.35.1 to 0.35.2, `activityOffer` and `activitySignup` 0.2.0 to 0.2.1: two descriptions only (`paymentRequested`, `paymentRequestRef`), with their catalogue keys (the two stale keys dropped), `l10n:build` run.

Schemas touched: `activityOffer`, `activitySignup` (descriptions and versions only). The pay screen (#805) touches no schema, so the two land in either order; this PR claims register 0.35.2.
