Third-party licence notices (aggregated build artifact)
=======================================================

A file carrying this identifier is not licensed work of its own. It is output
produced by a bundler — webpack's `extract-comments` step — which collects the
licence banners of the third-party packages that went into an adjacent bundle
and writes them out verbatim, one after another, into a single `.LICENSE.txt`
companion file.

Such a file therefore contains the licence texts and copyright notices of many
different upstream projects, each of which continues to apply on its own terms
to its own code. No single SPDX expression describes the file, and asserting
one would misrepresent every notice inside it. The notices themselves are the
authoritative statement; read the file to see them.

This identifier exists so that REUSE can account for these files honestly —
recording what they are and who holds the rights (the respective upstream
authors) — instead of forcing a choice between leaving them unaccounted for
and falsely claiming them as the packager's own work.

Removing or editing the contents of such a file may strip an attribution that
an upstream licence requires. Do not hand-edit them; they are regenerated by
the build that produced the bundle beside them.
