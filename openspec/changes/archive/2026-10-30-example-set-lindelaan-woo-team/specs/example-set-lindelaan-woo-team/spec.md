# example-set-lindelaan-woo-team

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Pieter Jansen of Woo en informatie handles the Lindelaan Woo case

The Zuiddrecht fragment SHALL assign the Woo case `woo-fietspad` to Pieter Jansen and to the group `zuiddrecht-woo-en-informatie`, and SHALL give him its tasks other than the one Mobiliteit en verkeer did. Its contact moments SHALL name him as the employee and Sanne as the resident.

#### Scenario: A handler opens the case
- **GIVEN** the set is installed
- **WHEN** the Lindelaan Woo case opens
- **THEN** the handling card SHALL read Behandelaar Pieter Jansen, Team Woo en informatie
